Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Halora is open source under the GPL-3.0 license. Support is welcome but never required.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Every conversion runs on your own PC with FFmpeg, ImageMagick and Windows' built-in PDF engine. Halora doesn't use the internet at all.

Doesn't Shift + drag move files in Explorer?

It does, so while Halora is on, Shift + drag opens Halora instead. Halora always answers "copy", so your original stays where it is. To move files, drag with the right mouse button and pick Move here, or switch Halora off from the tray. Letting go of Shift before you drop also cancels Halora.

Why does it need FFmpeg and ImageMagick?

They're free, trusted open-source tools that do the actual converting. FFmpeg handles video and audio, and ImageMagick gives the best image quality. The installer can set both up for you, and without ImageMagick images are converted with FFmpeg.

Will the quality drop?

Not where it doesn't have to. With ImageMagick, PNG, GIF, BMP and TIFF to WebP is lossless, JPG and AVIF keep full colour resolution, and colour profiles and camera data are kept. Formats like JPG are lossy by design, so Halora uses high quality settings for them.

Something doesn't work. Where do I report it?

Open an issue on GitHub. The tray menu's Open log folder shows a log you can attach.
